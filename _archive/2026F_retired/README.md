Material kept for reference only. Nothing in this folder is part of the live
Fall 2026 (QM47400) course arc, and nothing here is linked from the course
website, Brightspace, or the schedule.

| File | Retired | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `nb10_midterm_casebook_student.ipynb` | 2026-08-14 | The Fall 2026 midterm exam was dropped. The casebook existed only to prepare for that exam and to point at Final Project Milestone 02, whose full guidance now lives in `_final_project/2026F/milestone_02_expanded_outline.md`. |

The Fall 2026 arc is **20 notebooks**: `nb00`–`nb09` and `nb11`–`nb20`. There is
no `nb10` this term, and the numbering of the surviving notebooks is unchanged —
`nb11` still means Decision Trees everywhere it is referenced.

The midterm's 20% was redistributed: Course Case Competition 20% → 30%, Final
Project 35% → 45%. Fall 2026 weights are Participation 5% / Quizzes 20% /
Course Case Competition 30% / Final Project 45%.

The instructor copy (`nb10_midterm_casebook_instructor.ipynb`) is archived
alongside it but stays untracked, per the repo-wide rule that instructor
notebooks are never committed; the `.gitignore` in this folder enforces that
here, since the root rule is anchored to `notebooks/`.
