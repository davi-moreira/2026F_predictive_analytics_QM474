Poster-to-Product

From Poster to Product: Your Launch Journey

Three weeks. One decision-maker. One product you would show an employer.

Nov 16 – Dec 11three weeks, nine stages
5%of your course grade (100 points)
+5%Launch Award: top 3 teams in each section
Dec 9 & 11Demo Days, in class

After the Fall Undergraduate Research Conference, each team turns its poster into a product: one decision-maker, one decision, and a live dashboard that helps them make it. The method is The Launch Sprint, design thinking with a deadline: understand one decision-maker, sketch, decide, build a realistic prototype, test it with real users, then launch. Full instructions, rubrics, templates and videos are on Brightspace (Module 5: Poster-to-Product).

Demo Days

Half of the teams in each section present on Wed Dec 9 and half on Fri Dec 11, in a randomly drawn order (seed 474). Each slot is 6.5 minutes: a 2-minute live demo, a 3.5-minute debrief with Professor Moreira, and a 1-minute switch-over while the audience submits its review. Times are minutes after class starts. Come to both days: you review every other team in your section (I6), and your classmates’ reviews count toward each team’s Demo Day grade. The review link and QR code appear on the screen in class.

The Launch Award

  • Industry professionals and alumni review every team’s product from home, Dec 8–11, with the judging rubric you already have.
  • Teams are ranked within each section on the judges’ average product rating. Every member of the top 3 teams in each section gets +5% on the final course grade.
  • Ties are broken by Decision usefulness, then Evidence and honesty, then Clarity and ease of use; teams still tied all win.
  • To be eligible, submit a complete Launch Package on time and present on your Demo Day.
  • The award is separate from your grade: the judges never see your grades, and your grades never use the judges’ scores. Winners are announced by Mon Dec 14.
