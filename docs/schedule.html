Course Schedule

Fall 2026 — Monday / Wednesday / Friday. The 18-notebook arc is paced across the semester. Mondays and Wednesdays carry the course content; every Friday is a group work session for the final project and the Course Case Competition. Two Fridays are the exceptions: Fri Aug 28 carries nb02, and Fri Sep 25 hosts the in-class Midterm Exam — on paper, during class time, covering everything taught through nb09. Lecture content completes on Mon Oct 26, the Undergraduate Research Conference poster presentation is Tue Nov 17 (all students present, final poster due Tue Nov 10), and the Poster-to-Product build sprint runs through early December. Final-project milestone due dates are listed in the table below the schedule.

The Notebook column names the notebook (nbNN) anchored to each session — click the badge to open it in Google Colab. The Track column marks what the session is for: Content (new notebook material), Group Work (Friday sessions for the final project and the Course Case Competition), Exam (the in-class midterm), Poster (poster production and presentation prep), Competition (dedicated competition work sessions), P2P (the post-conference Poster-to-Product sprint), URC (the conference presentation), and Wrap (the final session). Sessions with — in the Notebook column carry no new notebook. Milestone due dates live in the Final Project Milestones table below rather than in this schedule.

Course Case Competition

The Fall 2026 QM47400 Case Competition (Bank Churn) on Kaggle opens Mon Aug 24 and runs the whole semester alongside the course. Friday Group Work sessions are dedicated time for the competition and the final project; the competition concludes with an online work session on Mon Nov 23 and a final submission deadline of Sun Nov 29, 11:59 PM — the Sunday of Thanksgiving week, and the last day submissions are accepted on the platform.
